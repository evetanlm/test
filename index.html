Good questions

Things people often wonder

What does "fiduciary" even mean?

It means we are legally and ethically bound to put your interests ahead of our own. We also do not earn commissions on products, which keeps our advice honest.

How do your fees work?

We agree on a clear fee in writing before any work begins, and we walk you through it in your first chat so there are no surprises.

Do I need lots of savings to work with you?

Nope. Many people come to us right at the start of their journey. Tell us where you are and we will honestly say whether we are a good fit.

What should I bring to the first chat?

Just yourself and a sense of what matters to you. Recent statements help but are not required.

How often will we check in?

Most people meet with us once or twice a year, plus whenever life changes. You can ask questions in between too.

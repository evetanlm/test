Questions

Frequently asked

What does it mean that Horizon is a fiduciary?

A fiduciary is legally and ethically bound to put your interests ahead of their own. We do not earn commissions on products, which helps keep our advice free from conflicts.

How are your fees structured?

We use a transparent fee arrangement agreed in writing before any work begins. We will walk you through the details during your introductory consultation so there are no surprises.

Do I need a large portfolio to work with you?

Not necessarily. We work with people at many stages, including those just beginning to build wealth. Book a conversation and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.

What should I prepare for the first meeting?

Just bring yourself and a sense of what matters to you. If you have recent statements or account summaries to hand, they help, but they are not required for the first conversation.

How often will we meet?

Most clients meet with us at least once or twice a year, with check-ins whenever life changes. You can reach us in between whenever questions come up.
